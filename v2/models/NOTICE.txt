3D cortical surface: fsaverage5, distributed with Nilearn 0.10.4 and derived from the FreeSurfer standard surface.
Source: https://github.com/nilearn/nilearn/tree/0.10.4/nilearn/datasets/data/fsaverage5
Files: pial_left.gii.gz and pial_right.gii.gz.
Reference: Fischl B, Sereno MI, Tootell RBH, Dale AM. High-resolution intersubject averaging and a coordinate system for the cortical surface. Human Brain Mapping 8(4), 272-284 (1999).

MODIFIED DISTRIBUTION: GIFTI arrays have been decoded into a compact binary file. Each hemisphere contains 10242 Float32 xyz vertices followed by 20480 Int32 triangle triplets, little-endian. No patient scan or identifying data was added. Presentation-specific coordinate transform, vertex colors and lighting are applied at runtime. This is an average cortical surface, not a complete or patient-specific brain. No functional connectivity or diagnostic inference is supplied by the source mesh.
